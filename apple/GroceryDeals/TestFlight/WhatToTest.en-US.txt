Thanks for trying Seattle Grocery Deals! It gathers this week's sales from Seattle-area Asian markets, seafood shops and co-ops, read from each store's own flyer, so you know where to shop before you go.

Things to try:
- Pick the stores you actually shop at, then check This week to see which one has the best week.
- Tap + on a few deals to build a list, or connect it to a Reminders list you share.
- Search for something you buy often, like pork belly or bok choy.
- Watch a price and get a nudge when it drops.
- Leave "New deals from your stores" on to hear when your stores post a new flyer.

New in this build: a smoother first-run setup (alerts start on, and you can skip any step on its own), and stores whose new flyer we haven't read yet now say so instead of saying it isn't out.

Good to know: it covers 13 Seattle-area stores, and each one updates as soon as its new flyer is out. Prices come straight from the flyers, so double-check in store.
