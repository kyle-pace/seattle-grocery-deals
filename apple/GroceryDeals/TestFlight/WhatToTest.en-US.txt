Thanks for trying Grocery Deals. It shows this week's sales at Seattle's Asian markets, seafood shops and co-ops, read from each store's own flyer.

Worth trying:
- Pick the stores you shop at, then see which one has the best week on This week.
- Add a few deals to your list, or connect it to a Reminders list you share.
- Search for something you buy often, like "pork" or "bok choy".
- Watch a price and see if you get a nudge when it drops.

Tell me what's confusing, what's missing, or a store you'd like added. Take a screenshot in the app to send feedback, or write to hello@seattlegrocery.deals.
