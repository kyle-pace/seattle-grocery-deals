Thanks for trying Seattle Grocery Deals! It gathers this week's sales from Seattle-area Asian markets, seafood shops and co-ops, read from each store's own flyer, so you know where to shop before you go.

New in this build:
- A shorter name on your Home Screen: Grocery Deals.
- Recipe ideas: swipe up on a deal for recipes from independent cooks that use it. Tap Something else for more. Store pages and your list suggest recipes too.
- Plan my trip: with two or more things on your list, the List tab shows which of your stores has the most of it on sale.
- Your list and stores stay the same on your iPhone and iPad through your own iCloud.
- Widgets: Best This Week (now following your stores, also on the Lock Screen) and your grocery list. Plus an Add to List control for Control Center and the Action button.
- Ask Siri: "What's on sale in Grocery Deals", "Plan my trip in Grocery Deals", "What on my list is on sale in Grocery Deals".
- Think a store's new flyer should be in by now? Tap "Think this is wrong?" under it.
- The heart now has a tip jar, if you'd like to chip in. Send feedback moved to Settings.

Things to try:
- Pick the stores you actually shop at, then check This week to see which one has the best week.
- Tap + on a few deals to build a list, then open the app on your iPad.
- Add a widget to your Home or Lock Screen.

Good to know: prices come straight from the flyers, so double-check in store.
